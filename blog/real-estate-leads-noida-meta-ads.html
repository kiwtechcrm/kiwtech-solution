Real estate leads in Noida: how to get site visits from Meta ads

By Kiwtech Solution team2026-09-268 min read

Noida's property market moves fast. New launches along the Noida Expressway, Sector 150 and Noida Extension compete for the same buyers, and most of them first see a project on Instagram or Facebook. Yet many builders and brokers tell us the same thing: "We get leads, but nobody books a site visit."

The problem is rarely the ads alone. It is usually the whole chain: who sees the ad, what the form asks, and how fast your team calls back. This guide walks through a setup that works for Noida real estate in 2026.

1. Target buyers, not browsers

A 2BHK in Sector 76 and a luxury 4BHK on the Expressway are bought by very different people. Running one broad campaign for both wastes money.

  • Location: target Noida, Greater Noida, East Delhi and Ghaziabad for end-users. Add Gurgaon and South Delhi for premium and investment projects.
  • Age: 28 to 55 works for most residential projects. Adjust for studio apartments or senior living.
  • Retargeting: build audiences from people who watched 50% of your walkthrough video or opened your lead form. These are your warmest buyers.

2. Lead with the video, not the brochure

Buyers scroll past brochure-style images with floor plans and small text. What stops them is movement and a clear hook in the first two seconds.

  • A 20 to 30 second walkthrough of the sample flat, shot vertically on a phone.
  • A drone or rooftop shot showing metro, school or expressway connectivity.
  • A simple text hook on screen: "3BHK near Sector 142 metro, possession 2027".

Run at least three creatives side by side. After four to five days, move the budget to the one bringing the lowest cost per qualified lead, not just the cheapest form fill.

3. Add qualifying questions to the lead form

This single change improves lead quality more than anything else. Instead of only name and phone, add two or three short multiple-choice questions:

  • Budget: Under ₹60 lakh / ₹60 lakh – ₹1 crore / Above ₹1 crore
  • Timeline: Ready to buy in 3 months / 3–6 months / Just exploring
  • Purpose: Self-use / Investment

You will get slightly fewer leads, but your sales team will spend time on people who can actually buy. Choosing Meta's "higher intent" form type, which adds a review step before submitting, filters out accidental clicks.

4. Call within five minutes

A buyer who fills a form is usually comparing three or four projects at the same time. The first developer to call often gets the site visit. After an hour, many leads stop picking up.

  • Send every new lead instantly to your sales team's WhatsApp.
  • Keep a short calling script: confirm budget, share one key benefit, and offer two specific visit slots.
  • Follow up missed calls with a WhatsApp message that includes the walkthrough video and location pin.

5. Use WhatsApp ads for warm audiences

For retargeting audiences, click-to-WhatsApp ads work very well. The buyer starts a chat directly, and your team can share floor plans, price sheets and the location immediately. Conversations feel personal, which matters for a decision this big.

6. Measure site visits, not leads

The number that matters is cost per site visit. Track every lead in a simple sheet with three columns: contacted, visit booked, visit done. After two weeks you will clearly see which ad, audience and form bring real visits.

For example, if Campaign A brings leads at ₹250 each but only 2% visit, and Campaign B brings leads at ₹450 each with 8% visiting, Campaign B is actually far cheaper per site visit. Without tracking visits, you would switch off the better campaign.

A simple weekly routine

  • Monday: review cost per site visit by campaign.
  • Wednesday: pause the weakest creative, add one new one.
  • Friday: check lead response time and follow up on unconverted leads.

Frequently asked questions

How much budget do I need for a Noida real estate campaign?

For a single project, ₹1,000 to ₹3,000 per day is a practical starting range for testing. Launch campaigns usually need more for a short burst.

Are Google Ads better than Meta ads for real estate?

Google Ads capture people actively searching, such as "3BHK flats in Noida Extension". Meta ads create interest among people who fit the buyer profile. Most projects get the best results using both together.

Can I pay only for leads?

Yes. Real estate is one of the industries where we offer a pay-per-lead model.
