Lead form vs WhatsApp vs landing page: which works best for Meta ads?

By Kiwtech Solution team2026-09-055 min read

When you run Facebook and Instagram ads for enquiries, you have three main options for where the customer lands: Meta's instant lead form, a WhatsApp chat, or your own landing page. Each works differently, and the best choice depends on your business.

Instant lead forms

The form opens inside Facebook or Instagram with the user's details pre-filled. It usually gives the lowest cost per lead because it is so easy to submit.

  • Best for: high-volume enquiries where your team calls every lead quickly.
  • Watch out for: lower quality, because some people submit without much thought. Adding a qualifying question or using the "higher intent" form type helps.

Click-to-WhatsApp ads

The customer starts a WhatsApp chat with your business directly. Indian customers are very comfortable on WhatsApp, and the conversation feels personal.

  • Best for: businesses that can reply fast, such as clinics, local services, e-commerce and education.
  • Watch out for: slow replies. If nobody responds within minutes, the lead goes cold.

Landing pages

The customer visits a dedicated page on your website with details, photos and a form. Cost per lead is often higher, but leads are usually better informed.

  • Best for: high-value services like real estate, premium treatments or B2B, where buyers need more information.
  • Watch out for: slow or cluttered pages. The page must load fast on mobile and have one clear action.

Our recommendation

Most businesses should test at least two options and compare not just cost per lead, but cost per actual customer. The cheapest leads are not always the most profitable ones.
