How much should a small business spend on Meta ads in India?

By Kiwtech Solution team2026-09-206 min read

One of the first questions every business owner asks us is: "How much should I spend on Facebook ads?" There is no single right number, but there is a right way to decide it. This guide explains how we plan budgets for small and medium businesses in India.

Start with what a customer is worth to you

Before thinking about ad spend, work out two numbers: your average profit per customer and how many enquiries it usually takes to close one sale. If a clinic earns ₹3,000 profit per new patient and closes one out of every four enquiries, then paying up to ₹750 per lead can still be profitable.

This simple calculation tells you your maximum affordable cost per lead. Every campaign decision after this is about getting below that number.

A sensible testing budget

Meta's system needs data to learn who responds to your ads. For most local businesses, a testing budget of ₹500 to ₹1,000 per day for two to three weeks gives enough data to see which audiences and creatives work.

  • Test two or three audiences, not ten. Too many splits the budget too thin.
  • Run at least three different creatives: one image, one carousel and one short video.
  • Do not change campaigns every day. Give each version three to four days before judging it.

When to scale

Once a campaign is consistently bringing leads below your maximum affordable cost, increase the budget gradually, around 20 to 30 percent every few days. Sudden large jumps can reset the learning phase and push costs up.

Budget is not the only lever

Many businesses assume more budget means more results. In practice, better creatives, a stronger offer and faster follow-up on leads often improve results more than spending more money. A lead that gets a call within five minutes is far more likely to convert than one called the next day.

Quick summary

  • Calculate your maximum affordable cost per lead first.
  • Test with ₹500 to ₹1,000 per day for two to three weeks.
  • Scale winners slowly, 20 to 30 percent at a time.
  • Improve creatives and follow-up speed alongside budget.
