Local SEO checklist for clinics and shops in Delhi NCR

By Kiwtech Solution team2026-09-127 min read

For most local businesses, the Google Maps listing brings more calls than the website. The top three map results for searches like "dentist near me" or "salon in Indirapuram" get the majority of clicks. Here is the checklist we use for local businesses in Delhi NCR.

1. Complete your Google Business Profile fully

  • Choose the most accurate primary category, and add relevant secondary categories.
  • Add every service you offer, with short descriptions.
  • Write a clear business description that mentions what you do and the area you serve.
  • Set correct opening hours, including special holiday hours.

2. Add real photos regularly

Upload photos of your storefront, interior, team and work. Listings with regular fresh photos tend to get more engagement. Avoid stock images; customers want to see the real place.

3. Build a steady stream of reviews

Ask every satisfied customer for a review, ideally on the same day. Share a direct review link on WhatsApp with a short, polite message. Reply to every review, positive or negative, professionally.

4. Keep your name, address and phone consistent

Your business name, address and phone number should match exactly across Google, your website, Justdial, IndiaMART, Facebook and other directories. Inconsistencies confuse Google and can weaken rankings.

5. Post updates every week

Google posts for offers, events and updates show your profile is active. One or two posts per week is enough for most businesses.

6. Support it with your website

  • Add your address and a Google Map embed on the contact page.
  • Create pages for the key areas you serve.
  • Add LocalBusiness schema markup to your website.
